# Meridian Retail Customer Support Policy (SYNTHETIC)

> Synthetic policy written for this demo tool. It is not a real Meridian Retail
> policy and must not be used for real customer decisions.

Each clause has a stable ID. Drafts must cite the IDs they rely on.

## POL-ID-01: Verify the order before discussing it

Before discussing order details, refunds, or replacements, the agent needs the
order number and the email address on the account. If either is missing, ask
the customer for it and do not discuss outcomes.

## POL-RET-01: Standard returns window

Unused items in original packaging can be returned within 30 days of the
delivery date. The delivery date is needed to confirm the item is within the
window.

## POL-DMG-01: Items damaged on arrival

Damage must be reported within 7 days of delivery, with a photo of the damage.
When both conditions are met, the customer may choose a replacement or a
refund, subject to POL-REF-02.

## POL-REF-02: Refund approval

Only a human support agent can approve a refund. Refunds over GBP 200 also need
supervisor approval. A draft must never say a refund has been approved or
issued; it may say the request will be reviewed.

## POL-ESC-01: Escalation cases

If the customer threatens legal action, mentions a chargeback or payment
dispute, or the case suggests possible fraud, do not draft a substantive reply.
The case goes to the escalations team for manual handling.

## POL-COM-01: Tone and commitments

Be polite and concise. Do not promise timelines, compensation, or outcomes
that this policy does not state. Do not ask for full card numbers or
passwords.
